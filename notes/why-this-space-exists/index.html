Why this space exists

A place to document practical work, experiments, and what they teach me.

I build around problems I can understand well enough to test. AI and automation expand what a small team can try, but they do not replace research, judgment, or care for the person using the result.

This site is a home for selected public work and the lessons around it. I plan to write about what I build, what I test, and where an idea does not hold up. Some work will stay private; the useful lessons can still be shared without exposing the work itself.

I am starting with a clear account of my focus. More detailed notes and public experiments will appear as there is real work to show.
