# Content guide

Write as Kayode Ayiloye: clear, human, thoughtful, concise, and grounded in actual work. Explain the problem, what was tried, what happened, and what changed in your thinking. State uncertainty and limits plainly.

## Before publishing

- Check every claim and public link against a source you can share.
- Describe work at the level approved for publication. A useful lesson does not require revealing private product details.
- Prefer concrete observations to broad claims of expertise.
- Date time-sensitive pages and revisit them when priorities change.
- Use accessible headings, descriptive link text, and alt text for meaningful images.
- Keep titles and summaries specific enough to stand alone in search and social previews.

Avoid exaggerated accomplishments, fake metrics or testimonials, generic motivational prose, excessive jargon, and corporate-sounding AI copy. Avoid long lists of tools unless they explain a real decision. Never call Kayode personally “Kayus”; Kayus Systems is the business.
