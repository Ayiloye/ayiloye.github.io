# Public brand brief

## Identity

- **Person:** Kayode Ayiloye
- **Positioning:** AI Builder & Product Founder
- **Business:** Kayus Systems
- **Relationship:** Kayode Ayiloye is the founder of Kayus Systems. The business is a secondary attribution on this personal site.

## Core statement

Kayode identifies real-world problems and uses AI, automation, and software to build practical solutions. He experiments with emerging technologies, builds real products, and shares what works, what fails, and what he learns along the way.

## Audiences and reputation

Prioritize founders and builders, followed by technical communities, potential customers, and ecosystem operators. Let credibility grow in this order: **products → research and experiments → open source → teaching**. Show the work before making claims.

## Public content pillars

1. Practical software and product discovery
2. AI and agent systems
3. Human-led automation
4. AI-assisted engineering
5. Selected experiments, research notes, and lessons

## Tone

Clear, human, precise, calm, and technically credible. Avoid hype, generic motivational claims, vanity metrics, and unsupported authority.

## Privacy boundary

Share lessons; protect the advantage. Reference only verified public repositories and approved public work. Describe private capabilities generically. Never publish private repository names, source, architecture, internal URLs, credentials, customer information, financial figures, or unpublished commercial plans. No private repository visibility changes, archiving, or deletion without Kayode's explicit approval.
